into a comma, colon or full stop. Please keep it that way.

"Read More" in a row sit on the same line. If you rewrite one, hold the length.

### Intro
**Heading** Three friends. *One shared purpose.*

Vera Vita did not begin with an opportunity. It began with a conviction. Senior living
in India deserves the long-term thinking that builds institutions. Three Hyderabad
families. Three different trades. One set of values.

A Chartered Accountant with an MBA from London Business School. His career runs through
audit, public markets, private equity and mobility. He brings the discipline of patient capital.

- **Experience** Saboo Group, Chief Growth Officer and Director · Mobility Impact Partners, New York · Stratford House, London · Purnartha · Ernst & Young
- **Education** MBA, London Business School · Chartered Accountancy, ICAI · B.Com, HR College
- **Focus** Capital allocation · Governance · Growth strategy · Healthcare and longevity

### A Note from the Founders
**Heading** Built for the people *we love.*

We began with one question. What should life feel like when there is finally time to live fully?

Seniors, families and experts gave us the same answer. Independent, connected and supported.
Never managed. That answer shapes every part of Amaya, and we would be proud to have our own
families call it home.

Signed: Dhruv Badruka · J. Arudradev Rao · Tanay Saboo, Founder · Vera Vita

### Institutions
**Heading** The institutions *that shaped us.*
**Lead** Enterprise, education, the arts, and a long view.

**Saboo Group** — Real Estate · Hospitality · Finance · Automobile

A Hyderabad group built over decades across real estate, hospitality, finance and automobile.
More than 200,000 sq ft of commercial development, and one of the city's leading dealership
networks. Built patiently, on relationships.

Stats: 200,000+ sq ft commercial developed · 15,000+ cars sold · 150,000+ serviced annually
Values: Trust · Integrity · Long-term thinking · Relationships · Operational excellence

**The Badruka Family & Northstar Group** — Education · The Arts · Real Estate

Badruka Educational Society has taught Hyderabad since 1950, with a Management School and a
School of Music and Dance. Northstar develops and manages residential, commercial and
infrastructure projects across the city, and manages more than half of what it builds.

Stats: Since 1950 educating Hyderabad · 4,000+ students each year · ₹140 Cr real estate held
Values: Relationships as bedrock · Nation building · Entrepreneurial spirit · Community
Inherits: From here, Vera Vita inherits the belief that relationships, not transactions, are the bedrock.

**Orient BlackSwan** — Educational Publishing · Since 1895

One of India's most respected educational publishers. It serves 14,000 schools across CBSE,
ICSE and State Boards, through a nationwide network and more than 1,000 teacher workshops a year.

Stats: 1895 established · 14,000+ schools served · 6M+ students each year
Values: Quality · Trust · Academic rigour · Educational impact
Inherits: From here, Vera Vita inherits quality, trust and academic rigour.
